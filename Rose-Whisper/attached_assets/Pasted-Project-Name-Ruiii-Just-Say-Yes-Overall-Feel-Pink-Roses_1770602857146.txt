Project Name:
“Ruiii, Just Say Yes 💘”

⸻

Overall Feel
	•	Pink. Roses. Hearts. No shame.
	•	Playful teasing, romantic confidence, zero hesitation.
	•	Feels like me talking directly to her, not an app.
	•	Cute + bold + emotionally sure.

⸻

Opening Screen

Soft pink gradient background. Floating hearts already moving slowly.

Text appears line by line (typewriter effect):

“Ruiiiiii…
okay listen.”

Small pause.

“I made this because asking normally
felt illegal.”

Button appears: “Okay I’m listening 👀”

⸻

Main Question Screen

Big centered card, rounded corners, subtle glow.

Text (large, soft handwritten font):

“Will you be my Valentine? 💘”

Below:
	•	YES 💗
	•	NO 🙄

⸻

NO Button Chaos Mode (This is where we GO ALL OUT)

Every time she taps NO, it:
	•	Slides away playfully
	•	Rotates slightly
	•	Sometimes shrinks, sometimes jumps
	•	Never disappears completely — just becomes annoying

Each attempt triggers your style messages (one at a time, above the question):
	1.	“Nice try.”
	2.	“That was cute. Try again.”
	3.	“Be serious for one second.”
	4.	“You don’t mean that and we both know it.”
	5.	“Ruiii stop lying to yourself 😭”
	6.	“Why are you like this.”
	7.	“Okay now you’re just playing hard to get.”
	8.	“This app is literally built against NO.”
	9.	“At this point, NO is embarrassing itself.”
	10.	“Just say yes before I add fireworks.”

Messages are short, teasing, confident, very you.

⸻

YES Button Evolution
	•	Gets bigger with every NO attempt
	•	Starts glowing more
	•	Hearts start orbiting it
	•	Slight bounce animation like it’s excited

After 5 NO attempts:
Text appears under YES:

“This is clearly the correct option.”

⸻

Midway Emotional Hit (Very You)

After enough NO attempts, background softens and this line fades in:

“You already know the answer.
I just wanted to make it cute.”

⸻

Final YES Click Moment 🌹

When YES is clicked:
	•	Explosion of hearts, roses, sparkles
	•	Background music softly fades in (romantic, dreamy)
	•	Card expands to full screen

Text appears slowly:

“Good.
Because I was going to be sad for like…
two whole minutes.”

Pause.

“Happy Valentine’s Day, Ruiii 💗”

Pause.

“I’m really glad it’s you.”

⸻

End Screen Buttons

Two playful options:
	•	“Come here 🤍” → hearts float upward
	•	“I knew it 😌” → screen does a tiny smug bounce

⸻

Extra Hidden Easter Eggs (Optional but elite)
	•	Long-press anywhere → text appears:
“Yes was inevitable.”
	•	Shake phone → hearts rain harder
	•	Screenshot detection → message pops:
“Aww you’re keeping this.”

⸻

Tech Requirements
	•	Mobile-first (phones > everything)
	•	Smooth CSS animations
	•	Touch interactions only (no hover dependency)
	•	Works flawlessly on WhatsApp/Instagram in-app browser
	•	Lightweight, fast, adorable
